04 · FAQ
Is it really free?

Yes. Free and open source, no paywall or account.

Is Showhow a new app or a fork?

It is a fork of OpenScreen, the open-source screen recorder created by Siddharth Vaddem and now continued by the OpenScreen contributors. Showhow keeps OpenScreen’s recorder and editor and adds a library of your recordings and the step-by-step guides. If you only want a recorder, OpenScreen is excellent on its own. Both are MIT licensed.

Does it upload my recordings?

No. Recording happens on your Mac. AI only runs when you connect your own OpenRouter key, and only for the guide step.

What if the AI step fails?

The recording always saves. A failed guide never loses a take.

Windows or Linux?

Not yet. macOS only for now.

Which agents does it work with?

Anything that reads text and a folder path, including Claude Code and Codex.
